```cfg
Suno提示词
* suno-game
    * 开启 Instrument制作无人声的BGM
    [1] 主题(主旋律)
        8-bit Chiptune(芯片)
        lo-fi : 低保真
        Music for a peaceful village in an 8-bit RPG
        Lead melody on a soft square wave synth
        A slow rock ballad. Starts with a delicate
    [1.1] 乐器
        looping bassline
        Simple arpeggiated chords on a pulse wave synth in the background
        A slow, steady beat from a noise channel drum
        # 吉他类
            Fingerpicked Acoustic Guitar : 指弹风格
            Percussive Fingerstyle : 敲击指弹风格
            Shredding Guitar Solo : 速弹吉他独奏
            Guitar Riff : 吉他连复段
            Power Chords: 强力和弦(充满力量的常用电吉他)
        # Baseline
            Funk bass
            Walking Bassline: 行进贝斯线 (在Jazz, Blues中常见，音符平稳地连接和弦)
            Fretless Bass: 无品贝斯 (声音平滑、有歌唱感)
            Deep Sub-bass: 深沉的次低音贝
        # 键盘类
            Grand Piano: 三角钢琴 (音色宏大、饱满)
            Upright Piano / Honky-tonk Piano: 立式钢琴/酒吧钢琴(旧旧的感觉)
            Cascading Arpeggios: 瀑布般的琶
            Delicate Piano Melody: 细腻的钢琴旋律 (轻柔、充满情感的单音旋律)
        # Drum Kit
            Acoustic Drum : 原声鼓
            Reggae drums : 雷鬼鼓
            Electronic Drum Machine: 电子鼓机 (如 808, 909)
            Snappy Snare: 清脆的军鼓
            Jazzy Brush Strokes: 爵士鼓(轻柔)
            Four-on-the-floor Beat: 四四拍
        # Strings 弦乐
            Lush String Section: 丰满的弦乐组
            Soaring Violin Solo: 高亢的小提琴独奏
            Dramatic / Cinematic Strings: 戏剧性/电影感的弦乐

    [2] 情绪
        Calm, Relaxing, Nostalgic(怀旧), meditative(冥想)
    [3] 应用场景
        video game music
        Sounds like a cozy, safe area in a classic Nintendo game

    [4] 节拍
        slow tempo
        60 BPM
```
